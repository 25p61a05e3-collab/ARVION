ARVION EVENT SAFETY SOP
========================

SECTION 1 — CROWD PRESSURE
Source: Crowd Management SOP, Section 1

If crowd density increases significantly or people begin pushing:

1. Immediately notify the event control room.
2. Stop or slow additional entry through the affected gate.
3. Deploy the nearest trained crowd-management personnel.
4. Keep emergency routes clear.
5. Use the public-address system to provide calm and clear instructions.
6. Monitor nearby gates for secondary crowd buildup.
7. Escalate to the event safety supervisor if the situation continues to worsen.

SECTION 2 — STAMPEDE RISK
Source: Crowd Management SOP, Section 2

If there is a suspected stampede risk:

1. Alert the control room immediately.
2. Do not send additional people into the affected area.
3. Open designated emergency routes where safe to do so.
4. Deploy trained crowd-management personnel.
5. Keep emergency responders' access routes clear.
6. Communicate calmly with the crowd.
7. Escalate immediately to the event safety supervisor.

SECTION 3 — MEDICAL EMERGENCY
Source: Medical Response SOP, Section 3

If a person is unconscious, seriously injured, or requires urgent medical attention:

1. Report the exact location to the control room.
2. Contact the medical response team.
3. Keep the area around the person clear.
4. Do not move the person unless there is an immediate danger.
5. Guide medical personnel to the incident location.
6. Record the incident after the immediate emergency has been handled.

SECTION 4 — BLOCKED EMERGENCY EXIT
Source: Emergency Evacuation SOP, Section 4

If an emergency exit is blocked:

1. Report the location immediately.
2. Deploy personnel to clear the obstruction if safe.
3. Keep the emergency route continuously accessible.
4. Redirect people away from the blocked route when necessary.
5. Notify the control room once the route is clear.

SECTION 5 — MISSING PERSON
Source: Lost Person SOP, Section 5

If a person is reported missing:

1. Collect the person's name and identifying information.
2. Record the last known location.
3. Notify the control room.
4. Alert designated search personnel.
5. Do not publicly broadcast sensitive personal information.
6. Record when and where the person is located.

SECTION 6 — FIRE OR SMOKE
Source: Fire Safety SOP, Section 6

If fire or significant smoke is reported:

1. Alert the control room immediately.
2. Notify the designated emergency response team.
3. Keep people away from the affected area.
4. Keep emergency access routes clear.
5. Follow the event evacuation procedure.
6. Do not allow untrained personnel to enter a dangerous area.
